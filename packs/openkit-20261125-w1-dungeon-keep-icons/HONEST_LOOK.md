# Honest look — dungeon keep icons (256 export, viewed at 32/48/64/96 light+dark)

Viewed the contact sheet at export size and a light+dark scale sheet.

## Strong

- **portcullis** — arch, rails, and pointed bars read immediately at 32px as a sliding gate, not a plain door or fence.
- **cresset** — basket on a post with tripod feet is clear at 32. Coals and the tiny flame need 48.
- **manacles** — empty open cuffs are obvious at export size and at 48. No figure, no blood.
- **arrow-slit** — a tall narrow slit in a stone wall face holds at 32. The masonry courses are clearer at 48. This is deliberate: a plain vertical slit in a wall, not a window.

## Weak at 32px (recommend 48)

- **key-ring** — keys merge into a cluster at 32; the split ring and mixed ward bits read at 48. Distinct from a single key.
- **grappling-hook** — at 32 it is a small claw and can still be taken for a compact hook or, less often, a small anchor. The diagonal pose, rope wrap, and three prongs read as a grapnel at 48.
- **piton** — a thin bent spike at 32; the eye and slight dogleg that separate it from a nail or needle read at 48.
- **murder-hole** — at 32 it is a dark opening in a trapezoid and can be taken for a vent or a well mouth. The soffit seen from below reads at 48. It is not a grate and has no ground parapet.

## Other notes

Manacles are empty hardware only. Coverage, visual-center, margin, and CairoSVG rerender gates passed (rerender maxdiff 0) after a coverage shrink on the portcullis and a centre fix on the grapnel.
