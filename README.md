# Open Kit

Free game assets we made. Download them. Use them in your own games, including commercial ones. No paywall.

This is **not** a Kenney pack. Kenney drew his by hand. These are made here (Grok Imagine and our own work). Same job: a pile of stuff you can grab without getting sued for a random web rip.

## Download

Grab the latest **Release** zip: `OpenKit-2d-screened.zip` (screened 2D pack only).

## What you may do

Use, copy, modify, and ship these files in games, tools, and other work, including for money.

## What you should still do

- Credit: made with Grok / Grok Imagine (xAI asks for that).
- Label: these are AI-made or AI-assisted. Say so on the download page / your credits.
- No warranty. If a file looks too much like someone else's character, do not use it. That risk is yours.

## What never goes in

- Kenney, Kaykit, Quaternius, itch packs, Unity store, or anything pulled off the web.
- Nintendo, Pokemon, Mario, Zelda, or other famous-character lookalikes.
- Anything our IP check rejected.
